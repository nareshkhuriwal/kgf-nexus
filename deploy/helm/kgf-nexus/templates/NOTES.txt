     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} wait --for=condition=complete --timeout=10m job/migrate-{{ include "nexus.jobSuffix" . }} job/bootstrap-{{ include "nexus.jobSuffix" . }}

   migrations shipped with THIS image tag are recorded; expected, not a hang.
